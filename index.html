Claude Drive Uploader

Claude Drive Uploader is a personal tool used by a single person, the owner of this site. It uploads video and audio files that the owner produces in their own work sessions to folders in the owner's own Google Drive, and publishes the owner's finished videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.
