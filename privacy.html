Privacy Policy — Claude Drive Uploader

Last updated: November 7, 2026 (YouTube API Services added)

Who uses this app

Claude Drive Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Google Drive API to upload files the owner created into folders the owner chooses in their own Google Drive.

The app also uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, captions and thumbnails. It may read the channel's own video list to avoid duplicate uploads.

It does not read, collect, or analyze any other content in the account, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

How data is stored and shared

Uploaded files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The app keeps no copies outside of it, and it does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone.

YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy

By using this app's YouTube features, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at Google Account › Third-party connections or the Google security settings page. The app stores no copies of Google user data; uploaded files and videos stay in the owner's Google Drive and YouTube channel.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/alibowbow.
